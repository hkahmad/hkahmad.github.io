I am a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science, Faculty of Computing, at Bayero University Kano (BUK), where I have taught since 2012 and currently serve as Faculty Deputy Dean. My research sits at the intersection of **recommender systems**, **graph representation learning**, and **educational data mining**, with a focus on using AI to personalise learning.

I received my PhD in Computer Software and Theory from Shaanxi Normal University, China (2023). My thesis, *Recommendation Methods in MOOCs using Graph Representation Learning*, developed course recommenders built on attributed bipartite network embedding ([ABiNE-CRS](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10489-022-03758-z)) and course-prerequisite relation embedding (PreBiGE). With collaborators, I have also worked on learning-style detection from learner behaviour using graph embeddings and graph neural networks (GRL-LS, GNN-LS).

This work matters especially in Africa, which has one of the largest out-of-school populations in the world. Personalised, data-driven learning systems can help widen access to quality education. I also work on responsible AI, including bias detection and mitigation in educational data mining, and collaborate with the [HausaNLP](https://hausanlp.github.io) group on NLP for Hausa.

At BUK I have taught postgraduate and undergraduate courses ranging from Advanced Computer Programming to Research Methodology. I have served as Level Coordinator and Postgraduate Coordinator, and I currently supervise MSc students working on fair ML for academic-performance prediction, fake news detection in Hausa, and hate-speech detection.
